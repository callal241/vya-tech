Stormvale: Wilder Realms

Support

Help for Stormvale: Wilder Realms on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Contact

Email privacy@vya-tech.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, and what happened just before the problem.

Saving and backups

The game autosaves every few seconds and whenever you leave the app. To keep a backup or move your hero to another device, open Menu → Settings → Export save and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the other device, choose Restore a save file on the title screen (or Import save in Settings) and pick that file.

Controls

Drag the left stick to move. Attack, abilities, guard and dodge are on the right. You can move and resize every part of the HUD under Menu → HUD. On iPad, a hardware keyboard also works. On Android, the back button closes menus and scenes, opens the menu while you play, and puts the game in the background from the title screen.

One hero per device

Each device keeps one active adventure. Export your current hero before you create a new one if you want to keep both.

Privacy

Stormvale collects no personal data. Read the Stormvale Privacy Policy.
