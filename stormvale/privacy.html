Stormvale: Wilder Realms

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy covers the Stormvale: Wilder Realms game for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by VYA Technology LLC. It applies to this game instead of the general VYA Privacy Policy.

Effective date: November 14, 2026

The short version

Stormvale is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores

Your adventure (hero, progress, settings and HUD layout) is saved on your device so you can continue playing. A second copy is kept in the app's own storage on the device, so your save survives if the system clears the app's web data. The app itself never sends this data anywhere. Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup), if you have turned it on, may include the app's data like any other app's; that is controlled by your device settings, and we never receive it.

Exporting a save

If you tap Export save, the app creates a save file and opens your device's share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Files, Google Drive, AirDrop or email). The app does not send the file anywhere by itself.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or online services.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone, and no special permissions.
  • No in-app purchases.

Purchases

Stormvale is a paid app. The purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies; VYA Technology LLC does not receive your payment details.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes all of its saved data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

For privacy questions, contact VYA Technology LLC at privacy@vya-tech.com. Help with the game is on the Stormvale support page.
